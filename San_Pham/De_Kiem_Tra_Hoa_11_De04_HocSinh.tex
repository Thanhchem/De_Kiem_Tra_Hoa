$\ce{NH4NO3(s)}: -365{,}61$; $\ce{NH3(g)}: -45{,}90$; $\ce{N2O(g)}: 82{,}05$; $\ce{HNO3(g)}: -134{,}31$; $\ce{H2O(g)}: -241{,}82$.
